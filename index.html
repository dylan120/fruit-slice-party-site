Camera-powered play on iPhone

Move. Slice. Play.

Use natural wrist movements to slice fruit, build combos, and turn every round into a cheerful party.

Designed for iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Four ways to play

Classic

Slice fruit freely with no bombs in the round.

Bomb

Build combos while staying clear of cartoon bombs.

Survival

Protect three shields and keep the round going.

Family Co-op

Work together through shared team challenges.

Private by design

Your camera stays on your device.

Camera frames are processed in memory for body and wrist detection. Fruit Slice Party does not record or upload camera video, and it uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Quick rounds, clear controls

Choose a 30, 60, or 90 second round, follow the five-second countdown, and review recent scores stored locally on your iPhone. Clear a safe play area before starting.
