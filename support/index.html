Fruit Slice Party for iPhone

Support

Fruit Slice Party requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later and camera permission.

Before you play

  • Place the iPhone in landscape orientation.
  • Stand where your upper body and wrists are visible.
  • Clear enough space to move safely and keep other people and objects outside your reach.
  • Wait for the five-second countdown before slicing.

If movement is not detected

  1. Open iPhone Settings and confirm camera access is enabled for Fruit Slice Party.
  2. Improve front lighting and avoid strong light directly behind you.
  3. Move far enough from the camera for both wrists to remain visible.
  4. Return to the app and retry the round.

Sound and vibration

Background music and vibration can be adjusted in the app's Settings. Sound also follows your iPhone's volume and audio configuration.
