Privacy Policy

Fruit Slice Party is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

Camera and body movement

The app requests camera permission to detect body and wrist movements. Camera frames and detected motion are processed temporarily in memory on your iPhone. They are not recorded, saved, transmitted, or uploaded to the developer or a third party.

Game history and settings

Round results and settings are stored locally on your device. Game history may include the round date, selected mode, completion status, score, and game metrics. It does not include camera images, pose points, identity, precise location, or device identifiers. You can remove this local information by deleting the app.

Data collection, analytics, and tracking

The app does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs that collect app data. It does not send game history or settings to a developer-operated server.

Purchases

Fruit Slice Party is offered as a paid download. The app contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple's own terms and privacy policy; the developer does not receive your full payment details.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children should use the app with appropriate adult supervision and a clear, safe play area.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be posted on this page.
